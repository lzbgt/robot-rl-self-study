# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — 2026-09-01

First complete self-study edition.

- Deepens all 20 chapters from reinforcement-learning foundations through
  robot control, Microduck training/deployment, imitation and offline learning,
  locomotion, vision-language-action policies, reproducibility, and capstones.
- Adds step-by-step derivations, plain-language interpretations, numerical
  examples, implementation mappings, research alternatives, and 2026 frontier
  signals with explicit evidence cautions.
- Expands the primary-source index to foundational papers, current preprints,
  and official open-source projects, separating reported evidence from artifact
  availability and hardware applicability.
- Provides 13 standard-library learning programs and 58 folded chapter-end
  answer or rubric sections.
- Publishes a 287-page A4 PDF with a semantic, accessible color system,
  embedded fonts, wrapped code/URLs/tables, complete answers, and a checksum.
- Adds automated checks for GitHub-safe mathematics, first-use abbreviation
  expansion, local links, example/catalog parity, chapter teaching depth, PDF
  contents, font embedding, contrast, and TeX overflow.
